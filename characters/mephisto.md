Mephisto is included in the NRCU Founders collection. The primary artwork is a pixel-art portrait with orange hair, closed eyes, a knit sweater, and a dark jacket in falling snow.

## Platform fighter adaptation

The playable adaptation pairs a young woman with her bonded shadow guardian. She leads the regular form, with the guardian close behind her. Her Zelda-inspired caster kit centers on reflection, a steerable sleep projectile, paired teleportation, and **Dream Hand**, the guardian's sleep catch.

The Story **Boss_Meph** direction retains that current kit. On the final stock, the guardian raises its supporting hand and carries her meditating on its palm; this presentation does not restore the old demon moveset.

See the game's [current character and kit notes](https://github.com/123mikeyd/nrcu-platform-fighter/blob/main/docs/MEPHISTO_CURRENT_DESIGN.md) for design versus implementation status. Game mechanics do not establish additional character backstory.
