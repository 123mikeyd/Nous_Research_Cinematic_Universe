Creator credit

Mike / 123mikeyd — NRCU co-artist. Doge Man, Super Shiba, and 123mikeyD share this character profile. Mike’s real creative credit is separate from Doge Man’s fictional fighting role. See co-artists & creative credits.

Character Overview

Doge Man's established game role is a close-range brawler. His signature attack sends him flying sideways into the fight like a torpedo: instead of firing a projectile, he becomes one.

Established Character Direction

  • Fighting style: close-range brawling.
  • Signature move: a flight-like, sideways torpedo charge.

These details describe established gameplay and animation direction, not a complete origin story.

Story Still to Be Defined

His origin and the source of his abilities remain open.
