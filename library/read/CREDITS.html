NRCU co-artists & creative credits

The Nous Research Cinematic Universe is a collaborative, fan-made character bible and art archive. These are real creative credits, separate from the fictional roles, powers, and allegiances in character profiles.

The co-artist credit does not make every artwork a joint work, transfer ownership, or establish an in-universe affiliation. Individual production and artwork credits stay attached to their sources.

  • Horizon: original FatCat artwork. Sahil’s collected style variations are credited separately; FatCat’s story role remains open.
  • Quark2World: the featured comic.
  • Sahil’s Hermes Multiverse collection: linked on the supplementary sheet pages. Inclusion preserves creative variations without automatically making them primary designs or adopting printed lore.

Reading the character bible

  • Character reference: the selected primary design, with approved supplementary art labeled separately.
  • Confirmed appearance: an identified appearance in a source video or scene, not an automatic change to the default design.
  • Community identity and creator work: real contributions, kept separate from fictional biography.
  • Open lore: details that have not been established. Missing stories and artwork stay open rather than being invented.

All Nous: the library is one alphabetical cast, not a hierarchy or a statement of squad membership. Gille and CoffeeBlender share a reading page while remaining separate identities.
